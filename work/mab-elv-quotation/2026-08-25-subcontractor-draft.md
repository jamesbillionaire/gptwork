## Commercial Conditions

1. All quoted prices are VAT exclusive.

2. The scope and quantities are based on the submitted MAB Electronic Plans and remain subject to coordinated shop drawings, technical submittals and site validation.

3. All electronic systems shall share one 42U MDF and four 18U floor IDFs. Separate CCTV, PA, nurse-call and CATV equipment cabinets and UPS units are not included or charged separately.

4. The shared MDF and IDFs include common rack power distribution, UPS protection, fiber termination and primary ELV pathways.

5. Equipment descriptions are performance-based. Exact models shall be finalized during technical submittal and approval.

6. Estimated duration is 90–120 calendar days from notice to proceed, mobilization payment, approved submittals and confirmation of site readiness.

7. Payment schedule:

   - 40% mobilization and procurement payment
   - 50% progress billing upon equipment delivery and substantial installation
   - 10% after testing, commissioning and turnover

8. Quotation validity is 30 calendar days.

9. Manufacturer warranties shall apply to supplied equipment. Installation workmanship carries a three-month warranty from project acceptance.

10. Additional quantities, rerouting caused by inaccessible pathways, revised layouts and additional civil or electrical work shall be treated as variation work.
