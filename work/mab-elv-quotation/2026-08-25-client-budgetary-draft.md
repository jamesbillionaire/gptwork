## Commercial and Technical Conditions

1. All quoted prices are VAT exclusive. Applicable VAT shall be added only when required under the final commercial agreement or billing arrangement.

2. This preliminary quotation is based on the submitted 34-page MAB Electronic Plans. Final quantities remain subject to approved technical submittals, coordinated shop drawings and physical site validation.

3. The drawing’s nonstandard “Cat6E” notation has been interpreted as standards-compliant pure-copper Hikvision CAT6 UTP cabling.

4. The electronic systems shall share one 42U MDF and four 18U floor IDFs. Separate CCTV, PA, nurse-call and CATV equipment cabinets and UPS units are not included or charged separately.

5. The shared MDF and IDFs include common rack power distribution, UPS protection, fiber termination and primary ELV cable pathways. Each system retains its required internal mounting, patching and termination accessories.

6. Equipment descriptions are performance-based. Exact equipment models shall be finalized during technical submittal and approval.

7. Third Floor Plan A and Third Floor Plan B have been treated as alternative layouts and counted only once.

8. The CCTV allowance includes a 32-channel recorder and 16TB raw storage. Actual retention shall depend on resolution, compression, frame rate, recording schedule and site activity.

9. The nurse-call solution uses an IP-connected management host and nurse-station hosts, with wired two-core signaling and power to field devices.

10. Estimated project duration is 90–120 calendar days after notice to proceed, initial payment, approved technical submittals and confirmation of site readiness.

11. Payment schedule:

- 50% down payment
- 40% progress billing upon equipment delivery and substantial installation
- 10% upon testing, commissioning and turnover

12. Quotation validity is 45 calendar days.

13. Equipment carries a one-year manufacturer’s warranty. Installation workmanship carries a three-month warranty from project acceptance.

14. Internet service, cable television subscription, recurring service charges, electrical panelboards, dedicated electrical circuits, major civil works, ceiling replacement, painting, permits and government fees are excluded.

15. Work resulting from revised layouts, inaccessible pathways or routes beyond the approved drawings shall be treated as variation work.
